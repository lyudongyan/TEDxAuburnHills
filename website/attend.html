Speaker Guidelines

TEDx speakers are never charged or paid. Talks are typically under 18 minutes and are developed collaboratively with the organizing team.

No selling

Talks cannot advertise a product, service, book, employer, business, or request for funding. The stage is for the idea, not a pitch.

Responsible claims

Factual and scientific claims should be accurate, transparent, and grounded in credible evidence. Political or religious advocacy and inflammatory rhetoric are not permitted.

Rights and release

Speakers must clear third-party material, work within TEDx copyright rules, and sign the TEDx Speaker Release before appearing on stage.

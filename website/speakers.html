Shelly Propson Lennon ↗︎

Shelly Propson Lennon has served as executive director of Paint a Miracle since the Rochester nonprofit opened in 2002. Its studio and gallery create inclusive arts opportunities for people with disabilities through classes, workshops, exhibitions, and the sale of artists' work.

Before Paint a Miracle, she practiced corporate and tax law at Bodman LLP, advising nonprofit and tax-exempt organizations. Her final talk title and connection to Retooling will be added after the program is confirmed.

Kaiqi Zhao, PhD ↗︎

Dr. Zhao is a tenure-track assistant professor in Oakland University's Department of Computer Science and Engineering and directs the Efficient AI Lab. Her research spans efficient AI, deep-learning model compression, speech recognition, and cloud and edge computing.

Her work develops smaller, high-performance, hardware-efficient models that can bring advanced AI to edge devices. Her final talk title and connection to Retooling will be added after the program is confirmed.

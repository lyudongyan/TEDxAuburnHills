Frequently Asked Questions

When is TEDxAuburnHills?

The event is Saturday, October 10, 2026, from 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM. Audience check-in begins at 10:45 AM.

Where is TEDxAuburnHills?

The event will be held at the Auburn Hills Community Center, 3350 E. Seyburn Drive, Auburn Hills, Michigan 48326.

How do I register?

Free public registration is open, with up to 100 places. Reserve your seat through the official attendee sign-up form. Registration includes event entry; food and drinks will be available for purchase.

How can I apply to speak?

Anyone may submit an idea through the speaker application on the Attend page. Applications close September 1, 2026, Anywhere on Earth. Applicants should be ready to collaborate on their script and prepare a working draft within two weeks.

Will the event be livestreamed?

Yes. The stage program is planned to stream free of charge. The viewing link will be added after the streaming platform is confirmed.

Will the venue be accessible?

The program is planned to include live captions for Deaf and hard-of-hearing guests. Additional access and arrival details for the Auburn Hills Community Center will be shared with registered attendees.

Can my organization partner with the event?

Yes. The Partners page identifies current production, accessibility, hospitality, and in-kind needs. Partners support the event but do not influence speaker selection or talk content.

When and where is TEDxAuburnHills?

Saturday, October 10, 2026, from 11:00 AM to 3:00 PM at Rochester Hills Public Library, 500 Olde Towne Road, Rochester, Michigan 48307. Audience check-in begins at 10:45 AM.

When will registration open?

Free public registration is planned to open August 1, 2026, with up to 100 places. Registration includes event entry; food and drinks will be available for purchase. The registration link has not been selected yet.

How can I apply to speak?

Anyone may submit an idea through the speaker application on the Attend page. Applications close September 1, 2026, Anywhere on Earth. Applicants should be ready to collaborate on their script and prepare a working draft within two weeks.

Will the event be livestreamed?

Yes. The stage program is planned to stream free of charge. The viewing link will be added after the streaming platform is confirmed.

Is the venue accessible?

The venue is wheelchair accessible, free parking is available, and the program is planned to include live captions for Deaf and hard-of-hearing guests. A private accommodation contact will be added before registration opens.

Can my organization partner with the event?

Yes. The Partners page identifies current production, accessibility, hospitality, and in-kind needs. Partners support the event but do not influence speaker selection or talk content.

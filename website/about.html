Retooling

Same hands, different tools.

Tools are more than objects. They include habits, systems, technologies, stories, and relationships. When circumstances shift, the tools a community inherited may need to be repaired, reconsidered, or replaced.

Retooling asks speakers to be specific about that process: what changed, what stopped working, what people learned, and which new method made a meaningful difference.

About TEDx

TED created TEDx so local, independently organized teams could bring people together around a TED-like experience. Live speakers and TED Talk videos create a starting point for deep discussion and connection. TED supplies general guidance and program rules; TEDxAuburnHills independently selects its speakers, shapes its program, and produces the event.

TEDx events are multidisciplinary, volunteer-led, and centered on ideas rather than commercial, political, or religious agendas. Speakers do not pay to participate and are not paid to speak.

Built for clarity, curiosity, and trust.

Ideas first

Talks center one clear idea and avoid selling products, services, books, employers, or personal brands.

Multidisciplinary

The program welcomes varied fields and perspectives rather than treating change as a single-subject problem.

Evidence and care

Speakers should handle factual claims responsibly, separate evidence from speculation, and build understanding rather than division.

More than a sequence of talks.

The afternoon is structured around two four-speaker sessions, live performance, an intermission with food for purchase, and forty-five minutes of community networking and photographs after the stage program.

Location, parking, and accessibility details will be shared once the new venue is confirmed. Live captions will support Deaf and hard-of-hearing guests, and the program will be livestreamed at no charge.

Designed for connection

Talks spark the conversation. The spaces between them help the ideas take root.

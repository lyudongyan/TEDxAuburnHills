Why Sponsor

TEDxAuburnHills is being planned as a free, in-person event for up to 100 guests, with a free livestream for people who cannot join us in person. Local support can strengthen production, live captions, speaker preparation, hospitality, food service, and community outreach.

Sponsors support the experience, not the editorial direction. TEDx rules require organizers to preserve independent curation and prohibit sponsors from speaking onstage or influencing the program.

Sponsorship Opportunities

Production

Stage lighting, audio, recording, livestream support, photography, and reliable event connectivity.

Access and hospitality

Live captioning, audience wayfinding, check-in materials, speaker care, and guest services.

In-kind support

Printing, equipment, professional services, and food or beverage service that directly reduces event costs.

Cash and in-kind support will be used only for legitimate event costs. TEDxAuburnHills is not a fundraising vehicle, and partners do not receive control over speakers or talk content.
